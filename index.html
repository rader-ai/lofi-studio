How it works

  1. Choose a vibe. Tap a vibe card and music starts. You can also type a description and hit BREW.
  2. Shape the sound. Drag the sliders while it plays: speed, bounce, how old it sounds, how much space.
  3. Pick instruments. Turn parts like drums or rain on and off.

Don't like the tune? Hit TRY ANOTHER for a new melody and beat with the same vibe. Love it? Hit RECORD, let it play, then hit it again to download the song.

Optional: open Add your own sounds to drop in your own audio, or Fine-tune for musical details.
