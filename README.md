# Lofi Studio

A lo-fi music studio that runs entirely in your browser. You pick a vibe and it plays, styled as a pixel-art castle.

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's nothing to install. Audio is generated live with [Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io/).

## How it works

1. **Choose a vibe.** Tap a vibe card, or type something like *"rainy night, soft piano, 72 bpm in A minor"* and hit BREW.
2. **Shape the sound.** Set speed, bounce (swing), vintage (tape dust and wobble) and space (reverb).
3. **Pick instruments.** Turn keys, bass, drums, melody, pad, vinyl crackle and rain on or off.

**TRY ANOTHER** keeps the vibe and makes a new melody and beat. **RECORD** saves what you hear as a `.webm` file.

## Albums

There are three finished albums, each with pixel-art cover art you can download as a 1200×1200 PNG:

- **Lanterns in the Rain:** late nights, rain on the glass
- **Sunday at the Observatory:** golden-hour jazz
- **Sleepwalker's Keep:** drifting, mostly drumless, after midnight

Each track is a fixed recipe and seed, so it plays the same song every time. Albums play one full arrangement per track, then move to the next track.

## Your own sounds

Drop audio files onto the page to use them as melody or beat loops (stretched to fit the tempo), vocal chops that follow the chords, background textures, or replacement kick, snare and hi-hat sounds.
